The siege record: ten redoubts built in 1861 and 1862 in a three-kilometre arc, rifled breech-loading guns emplaced on Broager Land on 15 March which Danish artillery could not reach, 1,150 rounds from the Broager batteries alone on 8 April, perhaps two or three hundred of them on the town of Sønderborg, parallels dug forward to three hundred metres, about eight thousand shells between four and ten in the morning of 18 April before the assault, and a counter-attack by the 8th Brigade that cost 1,399 of some 3,000 men. The casualty returns differ: Nationalmuseet gives 700 dead, the Dybbøl Banke history centre 1,669 dead and wounded together, Naturstyrelsen about 1,800 killed, wounded or missing, and danmarkshistorien about 5,000 lost in total. Most of the difference is in what they count.">
