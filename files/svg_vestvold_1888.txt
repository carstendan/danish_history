<svg viewBox="0 0 700 469" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" role="img" aria-label="Table of the Vestvold, the western rampart of the Copenhagen land fortification. Built between 1888 and 1892, fourteen kilometres long from Utterslev Mose to Køge Bugt, built under the engineer officer E.J. Sommerfeldt, with Estrup’s J.J. Bahnson as war minister, and its guns in concrete caponiers. About 3,150,000 cubic metres of earth and chalk were moved by local contractors and some two thousand workers, a third of them from Sweden, using shovels, wheelbarrows and tip-wagons, with the concrete mixed by hand because mixing machines did not yet exist. The whole land fortification cost about 13.2 million kroner between 1886 and 1894, by Bahnson’s own figures, and was paid for by provisional finance law against the vote of the Folketing. Further building stopped with the settlement of 1894; in 1909 it was decided to abolish the land fortification from 1922; fifty thousand men were called up to the Copenhagen fortress in 1914, and the security force was scaled down from 1915 and disbanded in 1919; no attack came; and it was abolished from 1 April 1920.">
